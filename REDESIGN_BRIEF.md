## Visual Identity

### Brand Palette
- **Primary:** Deep/intense teal (visually dominant)
- **Accent:** Intense orange (strategic use only - buttons, dividers, emphasis)
- **Neutral:** Cream/off-white (breathing room)
- **Supporting:** Black/charcoal (readability only)

### Design Standard
**Feel:** Premium | Professional | Clean | Minimal | Structured | Credible | Human

**Use:**
- Substantial negative space
- Strong typography
- Short paragraphs
- Restrained section lengths
- Simple visual hierarchy
- Professional product mockups
- Authentic photography
- Clean diagrams

**Avoid:**
- Excessive icons, emojis
- Unnecessary gradients
- Excessive animation
- Numerous cards
- Cluttered sections
- Generic consulting graphics
- Generic stock photos
- Overly decorative elements
