📋 What is the Launch Tracker System?

The LLI Launch Tracker System is your complete business launch management tool. It's a set of four connected spreadsheets that help you plan, execute, and track every step needed to successfully launch LLI Consulting Group on April 1, 2026.

🎯 System Purpose

Think of it as your launch command center. Instead of scattered to-do lists, notes, and reminders, everything you need is organized in one place with clear deadlines, priorities, and accountability.

The Four Components

Component What It Does When You Use It
1. Launch Tracker Your master task list (39 tasks) Daily - check what needs to be done
2. Friday Review Weekly progress check-in Every Friday - review the week
3. Prospect Tracker Your client pipeline (10 prospects) Daily - manage outreach and follow-ups
4. Message Templates Pre-written outreach emails When contacting prospects

How Long Until Launch?

⏰ Timeline: From March 13 to April 1, 2026 = 19 days

📅 Weekly Reviews: 3 Friday check-ins (March 13, 20, 27)

Who Does What?

Tasks are assigned to three owners:

  • CEO (You): Strategic decisions, messaging, leadership tasks
  • You (Operations): Research, prospect building, website updates
  • Shared: Decisions requiring collaboration

💡 Key Success Factor

These trackers work together as an integrated system. The Launch Tracker tells you WHAT to do, the Prospect Tracker tells you WHO to contact, the Message Templates give you HOW to reach them, and the Friday Review keeps you ON TRACK.

2️⃣ Friday Review (Weekly Check-In)

What It Is

A structured template you fill out every Friday afternoon to review the week's progress and plan for next week.

The Review Categories

Friday Review Template Structure

Section What You Track
📊 Dashboard Quick numbers: X tasks complete, Y in progress, Z overdue
✅ Tasks Complete List everything you finished this week
⚙️ Tasks In Progress What you're still working on
⚠️ Tasks Overdue Anything past its deadline (red flags)
🎉 Wins This Week What went well - celebrate progress!
🚧 Blockers / Risks What's stuck or concerning you
🤔 Decisions Needed Questions that need answers to move forward
🎯 Top 5 Priorities
Before Next Friday		 Your focus for the coming week

When to Use It

Every Friday at the same time (suggested: 3pm-4pm). Block this time on your calendar as sacred "review time."

Why It Matters

Without weekly reviews, you'll drift. The Friday Review forces you to:

  • Acknowledge what you accomplished (motivation boost)
  • Catch problems early before they derail the launch
  • Make course corrections while there's still time
  • Stay focused on what actually matters

The "HQ to Branch" Strategy

🎯 Strategic Approach

Step 1: Target headquarters (HQ) of large organizations

  • Example: Kaiser Permanente headquarters
  • Decision-makers have authority across entire organization
  • Bigger contracts, more impact

Step 2: After winning HQ, expand to branches

  • Example: Individual Kaiser hospitals, clinics, regional offices
  • Easier sell because HQ already uses your services
  • Creates multiple revenue streams from one relationship

How to Use the Prospect Tracker

  1. Start with Priority 1: Always work on your highest-priority prospects first
  2. Do your research: Find the decision-maker's name, email, LinkedIn profile
  3. Understand their pain: What keeps them up at night? This goes in "Primary Pain Point"
  4. Choose your angle: How will you position your services to solve their specific problem?
  5. Pick a template: Go to Message Templates and customize for this prospect
  6. Update status: As you move through the sales process, update the status field
  7. Set next steps: Always know what the next action is and when it's due

💡 Tips & Best Practices

Time Management

⏰ Recommended Daily Schedule

  • 8:00-8:15am: Review Launch Tracker - what's due today?
  • 8:15-10:00am: Work on high-priority tasks
  • 10:00-11:30am: Prospect research and outreach
  • 1:00-3:00pm: Continue task work
  • 3:00-4:00pm: Follow-ups, update trackers
  • Friday 3:00-4:00pm: Weekly review instead of updates

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Don't Do This ✅ Do This Instead
Work on whatever feels urgent Follow the Launch Tracker priorities
Forget to update statuses Update in real-time as you complete tasks
Skip Friday reviews when busy Protect Friday review time - non-negotiable
Send generic emails to prospects Customize templates with specific pain points
Let blockers sit without addressing them Escalate blockers immediately in Friday review
Work on low-priority tasks first Always start with high-priority launch-critical items

Warning Signs You're Off Track

🚨 Red Flags

  • 3+ overdue tasks: You're falling behind - need to reprioritize
  • Skipped 2 Friday reviews: You've lost visibility - get back on track now
  • No prospect emails sent in 3 days: Pipeline will dry up - send 3 today
  • Multiple "Blocked" statuses with no action: Escalate blockers to CEO
  • No wins in Friday review: Something's wrong - adjust approach

When Things Go Wrong

Problem-Solving Framework

If a task is blocked:

  1. Change status to "Blocked" immediately
  2. Note what you're waiting for in the Notes column
  3. Set a deadline for resolving the blocker
  4. Escalate in Friday review if not resolved in 48 hours

If you're behind schedule:

  1. Review all tasks - which are absolutely launch-critical?
  2. Consider moving medium/low priority tasks post-launch
  3. Ask: Can I get help? Can I simplify this task?
  4. Document decision in Friday review

If prospects aren't responding:

  1. Check: Did you customize the template with their pain point?
  2. Try different subject lines
  3. Follow up 3 business days later
  4. Try LinkedIn instead of email

Keyboard Shortcuts & Tips

  • Excel filtering: Use AutoFilter to show only "High" priority or "Not Started" tasks
  • Sort by deadline: Click "Deadline" column header to see what's due soonest
  • Color coding: Highlight overdue tasks in red, completed in green
  • Set reminders: Put critical deadlines in your phone calendar with alerts
